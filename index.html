How to read this score

Each item is scored Never = 0, Rarely = 1, Sometimes = 2, Frequently = 3, Always = 4, and the items are summed. Higher scores mean poorer sleep hygiene.

The four ranges above (0–13, 14–26, 27–39, 40–52) are simple, evenly spaced groupings used here for teaching. They are not validated clinical cut-offs, and the Sleep Hygiene Index does not diagnose a sleep disorder. Treat your score as a prompt for reflection, and compare it with your own earlier scores rather than with other people's.

If you have ongoing trouble falling or staying asleep, or you feel very sleepy during the day despite good habits, speak with a doctor or your university health or counselling service.
